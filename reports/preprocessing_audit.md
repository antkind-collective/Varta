- **Computed AFTER Text Normalization**.

## 5. Preprocessing Assumptions

1. **Payload Requirement**: A valid document record must contain at least a non-empty `title` or `text_content`. Exporter disclaimer notes lacking article text are non-documents.
2. **Syndication Deduplication**: Articles sharing identical normalized `title` + `text_content` represent duplicated news syndications across feeds. Retaining the first occurrence prevents redundant vector embeddings.
3. **Ghost Column Pruning**: Trailing un-named columns with 100% missing entries (`Unnamed: 5`..`24`) are CSV header formatting artifacts.
4. **Native Multilingual Preservation**: Multilingual text (Hindi, Bengali, English) must remain in native Unicode scripts to support downstream multilingual vector retrieval.
